All Our Available Trips

Find the perfect rafting adventure from our comprehensive list below:

1. The Mighty River Full-Day Expedition

Description: Prepare for an exhilarating full day on the Mighty River! This trip is designed for those who crave adventure, featuring a thrilling mix of Class III and IV rapids that will get your adrenaline pumping. Navigate through exciting drops and powerful currents, all while surrounded by stunning natural scenery. Our expert guides will ensure your safety and provide an unforgettable experience, culminating in a delicious riverside lunch. This is our most popular trip for good reason!

Difficulty: Intermediate to Advanced (Class III-IV)
Duration: Full Day (approx. 6-7 hours)
Highlights: Continuous rapids, scenic canyon views, hot riverside lunch provided.

2. Family Fun Float & Splash

Description: Perfect for all ages and first-time rafters, our Family Fun Float offers a gentle introduction to the joys of the river. Enjoy peaceful stretches of Class I and II rapids that provide just enough splash to keep things exciting without being intimidating. This half-day trip focuses on soaking in the natural beauty, with opportunities for swimming in calm pools and spotting local wildlife. It's an ideal choice for families with young children or anyone looking for a relaxing day on the water.

Difficulty: Beginner (Class I-II)
Duration: Half Day (approx. 3-4 hours)
Highlights: Calm waters, swimming spots, wildlife viewing, family-friendly fun.

3. Ultimate Wilderness Multi-Day Challenge

Description: For the seasoned adventurer seeking a true escape, our Ultimate Wilderness Challenge is a multi-day expedition into remote canyons. This immersive trip tackles challenging Class IV and V rapids, demanding teamwork and skill. Spend your nights camping under a blanket of stars on secluded beaches, enjoying gourmet meals prepared by your guides. This trip offers unparalleled solitude and a profound connection with nature, far from the hustle and bustle of daily life. Previous rafting experience is highly recommended.

Difficulty: Expert (Class IV-V)
Duration: 2-3 Days / 1-2 Nights (customizable)
Highlights: Remote wilderness, challenging rapids, riverside camping, gourmet meals, stargazing.

4. Adrenaline Rush Express


Description: Short on time but big on thrill? The Adrenaline Rush Express is your perfect fix! This fast-paced, half-day trip focuses purely on excitement, navigating a concentrated stretch of Class III-IV rapids. There's less downtime and more action, making it ideal for those looking to maximize their whitewater experience in a shorter period. Get ready for big waves, sharp turns, and a rush of pure exhilaration!

Difficulty: Intermediate to Advanced (Class III-IV)
Duration: Half Day (approx. 2-3 hours)
Highlights: Non-stop action, high-intensity rapids, perfect for thrill-seekers.

5. Sunset Serenity Float


Description: Unwind and reconnect with nature on our peaceful Sunset Serenity Float. This calm evening trip is all about relaxation and enjoying the stunning beauty of the river as the sun dips below the horizon. Gently float down Class I waters, perfect for quiet conversation, photography, and simply soaking in the tranquil atmosphere. Light refreshments are provided, making this a unique and memorable way to end your day.

Difficulty: Beginner (Class I)
Duration: Evening (approx. 2 hours)
Highlights: Peaceful floating, stunning sunset views, relaxing atmosphere, light refreshments.
